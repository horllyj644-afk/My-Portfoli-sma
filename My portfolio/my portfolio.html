About me

Hi, I'm John a 300-level Computer Science student of Bamidele Olumilua University of Education, Science and Technology (BOUESTI), Ikere-Ekiti, currently doing my SIWES placement at Maliyo Games in Lagos. I started teaching myself web design in June last year, and I picked up UI/UX design this July when i started MY SIWES — the two are my main focus now, backed by a growing interest in graphic design, video editing, and photography on the side.

I work full stack, using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and TypeScript, and I like being able to take a project from the interface design straight through to the code that runs it. Right now I'm building up a body of personal projects — design concepts I turn into real, working sites, just to prove they hold up outside a mockup.

What I believe

  • Good design explains itself. If it needs a tooltip to make sense, it isn't finished yet.
  • Fast beats fancy. A plain page that loads instantly is worth more than an animation that doesn't.
  • Ship, then polish. A real product in front of real people beats a perfect mockup every time.
